# Rules for Hermes, James and any other agent working on this repo

This is the public website for MoreHuman Ed. Treat everything here as public.

## How to work
- Open a pull request for every change. Never push to `main`. Diego reviews and merges.
- Pages are generated by `tools/generate-pages.py` (plain Python, no dependencies). Edit that file and run `python tools/generate-pages.py`. Do not hand-edit generated HTML. No other build tools, no new dependencies, no third-party scripts without Diego's approval.
- Do not change fonts, colours, the logo or the tokens in `assets/tokens.css`. They come from the MoreHuman Ed brand kit. See DESIGN-SYSTEM.md.
- Keep `/en/` and `/es/` separate. Never mix languages on one page.
- Match the voice in PLAN.md: warm, clear, practical. No em dashes, semicolons, hashtags or emojis.

## Never
- Include student, family or staff names, or anything identifiable. Use invented examples and say they are invented.
- Mention or imply anything from Diego's employer, its systems, students, staff or branding.
- Promise a number of hours saved or any outcome. Say what was measured and how.
- Publish testimonials or case studies without written approval from the school.
- Change prices, promises or privacy statements. Propose them in the PR description and wait.
- Contact schools, principals or families.
- Store passwords or tokens in the repo.
- Change hosting, DNS, the custom domain or search-engine settings. Only Diego does that.

## Good first tasks
- Draft a tutorial page from a video transcript Diego provides.
- Check links and spelling. Report broken links in a PR description.
- Draft LinkedIn posts in `drafts/` for Diego to edit. Do not post them.
