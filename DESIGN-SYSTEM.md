# MoreHuman Ed design system (shared with Discovery Lab)

Concept: editorial human systems. A calm studio, not a tech startup. MoreHuman Ed is the parent brand. Discovery Lab is another room in the same house.

## What is shared (lifted from Discovery Lab)
- Fonts, self-hosted: Fraunces (headings), Public Sans (body), JetBrains Mono (labels and data). Files in `assets/fonts/`.
- Warm paper background with a faint 48px grid: `--paper #F2EFE6`, grid line `--line`.
- Ink `#1D211C`, forest green accent `#40634C`, rust signal `#B5551F`, blue `#2F5871` (focus only).
- Hairline rules, 12px cards, 6px buttons, no heavy shadows.
- Orientation motif: ring, tick marks, one orange dot.
- Motion tokens: `--dur-*`, `--ease`, and `--motion` which reduced-motion sets to 0.

## What is different
- Discovery Lab's compass has an arc and three rings. MoreHuman Ed's mark is simpler: two rings, a centre dot (the teacher), and a line to one orange dot (the student noticed).
- MoreHuman Ed uses short scroll reveals; Discovery Lab stays an instrument-like workspace.

## To keep Discovery Lab in sync
Copy `assets/tokens.css` and `assets/fonts/` into Discovery Lab's `engine/` and map its tokens to these names. Do not change the fonts or colours on one site without the other.

## Rules
- No robot imagery, neon gradients, stock photos, invented testimonials or numbers.
- Content is visible without JavaScript. Scroll reveals respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Pages are generated by `tools/generate-pages.py`. Edit that file and rerun it.
