## Core decisions (from ChatGPT review and Claude review)
1. Buyer purchases teacher time, attention and consistency. Not AI. Agents stay backstage.
2. Beachhead: small independent and international schools. Not large public systems.
3. First offer: Teacher Time & Attention Pilot (price to be confirmed by Diego, see pilot page).
4. Personal LinkedIn first, company page later. Agents never get LinkedIn passwords.
5. Bilingual structure from day one: /en/ and /es/. Launch full English, Spanish home and pilot only.
6. Lead magnet: Teacher Time Audit. Newsletter: Teacher Time Notes. Consent-based mailing platform needed.
7. Privacy and safety page is part of the offer.
8. Name stays MoreHuman Ed for now. Run trademark search (IPONZ, IP Australia, USPTO, UKIPO, EUIPO, WIPO) before spending on branding. Fallback: Room to Notice Education.
9. Productise after three pilots: Teacher Time Operating System.
