# MoreHuman Ed: purpose and shape

MoreHuman Ed is a personal, independent place where Diego shares tips, ideas and reflections about learning, technology and keeping people at the centre of education.

## What it is
- A calm home for short notes, videos and reading reflections.
- Written in a humble, curious voice: "I'm exploring", "what I'm noticing", "something I've been learning".
- English first, with Spanish alongside.

## What it is not
- Not a business, a consultancy or a training provider.
- Nothing is sold or offered. No booking, no enquiries, no sign-ups, no tracking.

## Themes
AI and human intelligence in education; human connection and learning; evidence and research; teacher wellbeing and workload; student independence and critical thinking; systems thinking and improvement.

## Publishing
About one useful piece a month. No deadlines, no pressure to publish often.

## Rules for any content
- Invented or public examples only, and say so. No real learner, family or colleague information.
- Nothing taken from anyone else's systems, materials or branding.
- No claims about outcomes or hours saved without a source.
- Anything uncertain waits for review.

See DESIGN-SYSTEM.md for the look and AGENTS.md for how changes are made.
