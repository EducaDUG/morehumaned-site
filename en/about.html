About

Science educator and learning-systems designer.

Technology that gives teachers and trainers the time and energy to notice every learner.

Diego Guevara is a science educator, teacher trainer and learning-systems designer who helps schools and training organisations use practical technology to reduce routine work without losing the human heart of teaching and learning.

Drawing on experience across science, ecology, education and international online learning, he creates clear workflows that give teachers and trainers more time and mental space to notice and support every learner.

Through MoreHuman Ed, Diego explores how technology can strengthen human judgement, connection and care.

Diego is based in Dunedin, New Zealand, and works in English and Spanish.

He works from the classroom outward: testing small changes, supporting teachers as they use them, and checking honestly whether they improve the working day. The aim is not to automate the relationship between teachers and students. It is to protect it.

Side project: Diego also builds Discovery Lab, a separate collection of interactive simulations.
