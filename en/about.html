About

A teacher, not a programmer.

I care about teachers having the time and energy to notice every student.

I am an independent educator based in Dunedin, New Zealand. I work in English and Spanish.

I work from the classroom outward. I test small changes, support teachers as they use them, and check honestly whether they improve the working day.

The aim is not to automate the relationship between teachers and students. It is to protect it.

Side project: Discovery Lab is a collection of interactive simulations I build separately.
