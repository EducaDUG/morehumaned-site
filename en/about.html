About

A science educator, thinking out loud.

I'm exploring how technology can strengthen the human side of education.

I'm a science educator. I teach students of different ages and subjects, and I'm fascinated by how the principles of learning connect across disciplines and generations.

My background is in science and ecology, and I have worked in teaching, leadership and educational technology.

What I keep noticing is that technology helps most when it protects time and attention for people. This site is where I explore that: what seems to work, what doesn't, and what the research says.

I work in English and Spanish.

I also build Discovery Lab, a free collection of interactive science simulations for my students.

This is a personal, independent project. Nothing here is for sale and I am not offering services. You can read more on About this site.
