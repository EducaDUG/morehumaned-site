Practical systems for schools and training organisations

Give teachers and trainers more time and mental space to notice every learner.

MoreHuman Ed helps schools, training organisations and educators turn repetitive work and scattered technology experiments into practical, safe systems, so teachers and trainers can listen, notice and support each learner.

By Diego Guevara, science educator and learning-systems designer.

Who it is for

For people who teach, train and lead learning

01

Schools and school leaders

Principals, deputy heads and teaching teams who want to give teachers time back and keep the human side of teaching strong.

02

Training organisations and institutions

Training providers, vocational and professional institutes and corporate learning teams who want practical systems that save trainer time without lowering quality.

03

Trainers and adult educators

Independent trainers, facilitators and lecturers who want clearer workflows, better feedback habits and more time to notice the people in the room.

The problem

One approach for everyone fits no one.

Every classroom and every training room holds people who learn in different ways. Teaching to each of them is hard when teachers and trainers are tired and the day is full of routine work.

The answer is not asking teachers and trainers to do more. It is removing what does not need a human, so what does can have room.

The blanket approach

Everyone receives the same thing. Differences go unseen.

When teachers have room

Each learner is noticed and supported in the way that fits them.

The approach

Three ideas behind the work

01

Reduce routine work

Find the repetitive tasks that crowd out attention, and turn them into simple, repeatable workflows.

02

Protect professional judgement

Technology drafts. Teachers and trainers review, decide, and speak with learners and families. Nothing leaves the organisation without a human.

03

Make room for each learner

Time and mental space are the real resource. The aim is to give them back, so teachers and trainers can notice, listen and respond.

How it works

From a busy week to one useful change

  1. Understand your context

    A short conversation about your people, your priorities and where time leaks.

  2. Choose a focused workflow

    We pick one or two practical changes that fit, rather than trying to change everything.

  3. Support implementation

    Teachers and trainers try them in real work, with help close at hand.

  4. Measure what changed

    Confidence, use and estimated time saved are reviewed honestly, including what was hard.

Evidence and trust

Honest evidence, careful with data

What we measure

  • Teacher and trainer confidence, before and after
  • How often the workflows are used
  • Estimated time saved
  • How it feels for attention to learners
  • What was hard to implement

Privacy and safety

  • No learner-identifiable data needed
  • Demonstrations use invented examples
  • Teachers and trainers approve everything before use
  • Case studies only with written approval
