Practical systems for schools

Give teachers more time and mental space to notice every student.

MoreHuman Ed helps schools turn repetitive work and scattered technology experiments into practical, safe systems, so teachers can listen, notice and support each learner.

Explore workshops →Watch a tutorial

By Diego Guevara, science educator and learning-systems designer.

The problem

One approach for everyone fits no one.

Every class holds students who learn in different ways. Teaching to each of them is hard when teachers are tired and the day is full of routine work.

The answer is not asking teachers to do more. It is removing what does not need a human, so what does can have room.

The blanket approach

Everyone receives the same thing. Differences go unseen.

When teachers have room

Each student is noticed and supported in the way that fits them.

The approach

Three ideas behind the work

01

Reduce routine work

Find the repetitive tasks that crowd out attention, and turn them into simple, repeatable workflows.

02

Protect teacher judgement

Technology drafts. Teachers review, decide, and speak to students and families. Nothing leaves the school without a human.

03

Make room for each student

Time and mental space are the real resource. The aim is to give them back, so teachers can notice, listen and respond.

How it works

From a busy week to one useful change

  1. Understand your context

    A short conversation about your staff, your priorities and where time leaks.

  2. Choose a focused workflow

    We pick one or two practical changes that fit, rather than trying to change everything.

  3. Support implementation

    Teachers try them in real work, with help close at hand.

  4. Measure what changed

    Confidence, use and estimated time saved are reviewed honestly, including what was hard.
