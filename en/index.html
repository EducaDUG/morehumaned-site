One approach for everyone does not work

Every class holds students who learn in different ways and need different things. Teaching to each of them is hard when teachers are tired and the day is full of repetitive work.

The answer is not asking teachers to do more. It is to remove the repetitive work, so the human part of teaching has room to breathe.

How it works

  1. Notice. Find where time and attention leak during a normal week.
  2. Choose. Pick two practical workflows that fit your school.
  3. Try. Use them for 14 days, with support.
  4. Review. Look at what changed and what was hard. Keep what helps.

What stays the same

  • Human judgement stays central. Technology drafts, teachers decide.
  • No student-identifiable data is needed.
  • Small, reversible steps.
  • Honest measurement, with no promised numbers.
