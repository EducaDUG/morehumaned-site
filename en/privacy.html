About this site

A personal, independent project.

Nothing here is for sale.

  • No services are offered, and there is nothing to book or sign up for.
  • Examples are invented, and I say so. No learner, family or colleague information appears.
  • I use AI to help draft and organise, and I read, check and edit everything myself.
  • I try to cite sources, and to say what I'm unsure about.
  • The views here are my own.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no tracking and no forms. The video player loads from YouTube, in its privacy-enhanced mode, only if you press play. Nothing is loaded from YouTube before that.
