Privacy and safety

Careful by default.

Schools hold sensitive information. The way I work is designed to need none of it.

  • No student names or identifiable records in demonstrations. I use invented examples and say so.
  • No autonomous contact with students or families. Technology drafts, people decide and send.
  • Human approval before anything is published outside the school.
  • Clear handling of any school information: it is only used for the agreed purpose and is not shared.
  • A review of any third-party tool before it is suggested, and a privacy impact assessment if a system needs personal information.
  • Case studies and testimonials only with the school's written approval.

This approach is designed with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 in mind.
