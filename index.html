My purpose

Let machines do the machine work, and let people do the human work.

Much of a teacher's day is forms, formatting and repetition, and none of it needs a human heart. Technology can help carry that weight. What it can't do is notice the quiet student, listen with patience, or make someone feel seen. I'm exploring how to give teachers back the energy and attention for exactly that. My purpose is simple: to help as many people as I can learn, connect and grow, and to share what I learn along the way.

A question I keep asking

One approach for everyone fits no one.

Every classroom holds people who learn in different ways. Noticing each of them is hard when teachers are tired and the day is full of routine work.

I don't think the answer is asking teachers to do more. I'm exploring how to remove what does not need a human, so what does can have room.

The blanket approach
When teachers have room

Everyone receives the same thing. Differences go unseen.

Each learner is noticed and supported in the way that fits them.

Ideas I return to

Three ideas

01

Reduce routine work

Repetitive tasks crowd out attention. I'm curious about what can be simplified safely, and what should not be.

02

Protect professional judgement

Technology can draft. People review, decide and speak with learners and families.

03

Make room for each learner

Time and mental space are the real resource. I'm exploring how to give some back.
